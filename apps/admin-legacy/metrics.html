Central Metrics
—
Single source of truth. Every other page (Capacity, Audience, Health, Mailboxes) reads from here. Lead counts come from revenueCache (Revenue page = original source; PlusVibe /lead/workspace-leads?label=X for LEAD/MEETING_BOOKED/MEETING_COMPLETED/CLOSED/ADDED_TO_ZOHO/AWAITING_REPLY/WEAK_LEAD, minus user-marked non-leads). Sends from email_events + campaignCache.totalSent. Mailboxes from _mailboxCache (PV /account/email-accounts). Avg/mo = lifetime ÷ days_active × 30 (days_active = earliest of: client created_at, first lead date).
