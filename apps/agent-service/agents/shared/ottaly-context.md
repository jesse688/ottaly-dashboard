# Ottaly — Shared Business Context

## What Ottaly is
Ottaly is a UK B2B cold email lead generation agency. We run outbound email campaigns for clients across all commercial industries. We find the right prospects, verify emails, write sequences, and book meetings for our clients.

## How we work
- We use PlusVibe as our email sequencing platform
- We source leads from Apollo.io (977k+ contacts in our database)
- We verify emails with Reacher (SMTP verification)
- We manage 28+ client workspaces
- Campaigns typically run multi-step email sequences targeting decision makers

## Our clients
- B2B companies across all commercial industries
- UK-focused but not exclusively
- Range from startups to established businesses
- We manage everything end-to-end for them

## Our team
- Jesse — founder, runs the business
- Campaign Managers (CMs) — manage day-to-day client campaigns

## Our tech stack
- PlusVibe — email sequencing
- Apollo.io — lead sourcing (2 accounts: London+SE and Rest of UK)
- Reacher — email verification
- PostgreSQL — our contact database
- Admin dashboard at admin.ottaly.co.uk (legacy) and dev.ottaly.co.uk (new)

## Key metrics we track
- Sends, replies, reply rate, bounce rate
- Positive replies (INTERESTED, MEETING_BOOKED)
- Leads per campaign
- Reply-to-lead ratio (RTL)
